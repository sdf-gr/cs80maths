# cs80maths performance presets

38 original patches. The first eight retain their names, settings and order. The 30 additions are inspired by cinematic and classic CS-80 textures, not transcriptions of commercial recordings or measured factory resistor settings.

- **Bronze Sky** — Warm cinematic brass; pressure brings out the brighter upper layer.
- **Rainlit Lead** — Narrow pulse lead with a soft saw underneath; add pressure for vibrato.
- **Starship Swell** — Slow octave brass bloom for wide held chords.
- **Oceanic Choir** — Soft fifths with slowly moving vowel-like filtering.
- **Spiral Pulse** — Rhythmic square-wave filter motion with pressure-controlled pitch movement.
- **Antarctic Pad** — Airy slow strings with a little filtered noise and long release.
- **Dusk Fanfare** — Bright, quick brass with a softer octave foundation.
- **Celestial Bells** — Soft tuned bells with a restrained shared ring carrier.
- **Night Harbor Bass** — Deep rounded pulse bass with a dark delayed upper bloom.
- **Silver Ribbon** — Singing octave lead for expressive ribbon bends.
- **Warmth of Earth** — Low warm strings that open under pressure.
- **Horizon Solo** — Focused brass lead with a strong initial pitch scoop.
- **Cosmic Fifths** — Broad fifth-layer strings with gentle stereo movement.
- **Metallic Orbit** — Ring-modulated pluck whose metallic edge falls after each new phrase.
- **Gentle Memory** — Quiet rounded sine and pulse pad for understated chords.
- **Dawn Orchestra** — Layered string-and-brass swell, with brightness responding to pressure.
- **Classic String I** — Classic-style pulse strings with slow PWM and a restrained second layer.
- **Classic String II** — Brighter saw-and-pulse ensemble with a faster onset.
- **Classic Brass I** — Rounded classic-style saw brass with a clear filter attack.
- **Classic Brass II** — Brighter layered brass with more pulse edge and pressure response.
- **Breathy Flute** — Soft flute-like pulse tone with a trace of breath noise.
- **Velvet Electric Piano** — Velocity-sensitive mellow keys with a sine-rich decay.
- **Percussive Clav** — Dry narrow-pulse keyboard with short release and touch-sensitive bite.
- **Baroque Harpsichord** — Bright plucked keyboard with octave reinforcement and little sustain.
- **Cathedral Organ** — Steady pulse and sine registration with octave weight.
- **Plucked Guitar** — A short pulse pluck with a warm sine body.
- **Resonant Funk** — Snappy high-pass/low-pass pulse bite for rhythmic playing.
- **Rounded Bass** — Short, low pulse bass with sine support and a gentle filter fall.
- **Tremolo Keys** — Mellow electric keys with the shared stereo tremolo engaged.
- **Reed and Strings** — A reed-like pulse over a softer slow string layer.
